### policy
"One footprint per reference. No dual footprints, no coincident SMD/THT pad stacks, no option-pad sets. Where a part exists in both a through-hole and an SMD package, the through-hole socketed variant is the only one placed: U1 MCP6002 PDIP-8, U3 6N137 DIP-8, U5 TL431 TO-92, Q1 2N2222 TO-92, U4 LM35 TO-92, U11 DS18B20 TO-92, D1 1N4744 DO-41. Everything else is its single SMD package. All passives 0805 except the four forced exceptions."
